I'm Wasaf Abbasi. I work on Windows endpoints, identity, certificates and the operational plumbing around them, and I publish the tools I wish I had as open source on [GitHub](https://github.com/Abbasi26).

The tools share a few rules:

- **Read-only first.** Anything that changes a system or sends data needs an explicit switch.
- **Evidence over claims.** Reports cite their sources and mark what is unverified.
- **Privacy by default.** Identifiers are pseudonymised or fingerprinted where reports might be shared.
- **Try before you trust.** Each repository has a one-command offline demo with fictional data.

Issues, ideas and pull requests are welcome in any of the repositories. Every repository has a `ROADMAP.md` with good first issues.

New posts are in the [RSS feed](/index.xml).
